About

Turning ordinary clips into cinematic moments

Hi, I'm Nevin — a passionate video editor specialising in cinematic storytelling and high-quality motion design.

I enjoy transforming ordinary clips into visually stunning edits using industry-leading software and creative techniques: precise beat-matched cutting, layered transitions, colour grading and typography that carries the mood of a scene.

Whether it's character edits, cinematic reels or social media content, my goal is always the same — to create edits that leave a lasting impression long after the last frame.
